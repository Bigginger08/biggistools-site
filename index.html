What is BiggiTools?

A small, unofficial lab of browser-based tools—mostly around color management, printing, and everyday workflow headaches. Think of it as a sandbox where ideas show up before they become “real products”.

Tools might be rough around the edges, but the goal is always the same: save a few minutes here and a few clicks there for people who live in prepress, print, and color land.

Roadmap-ish

  • More color tools (charts, conversions, QC helpers)
  • Quality-of-life utilities for support & troubleshooting
  • Better documentation & examples once things stabilize
